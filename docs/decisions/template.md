# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

Status: Proposed

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Describe the concrete requirement and affected boundary. Separate facts and
experiment results from assumptions. Identify constraints, stakeholders, and
the existing ADRs this decision depends on or may supersede.

## Decision

State one actionable decision, its scope, and its non-goals. Identify the
responsible owner and independent reviewer. Record the acceptance/review
reference when approved; do not claim approvals that have not occurred.

## Alternatives considered

Compare the simplest viable alternative, retaining the current approach or
deferring the decision, and other credible options. Explain the evidence and
trade-offs that favor the chosen option instead of merely listing technologies.

## Consequences

Explain benefits, costs, operational/security/licensing risks, and limits.
Identify migration or rollback implications, unresolved gates, and conditions
that should trigger reconsideration.

## Validation

Specify commands, test cases, experiment artifacts, and measurable acceptance
criteria. Distinguish checks already run from future gates. For pending work,
identify the responsible role and the merge or deployment condition it blocks.
For behavior changes, link the MR's red/green/refactor evidence.

## References

Link related ADRs, the relevant PLAN sections, authoritative sources, and
observed evidence. Include version/date information for changing SDK or platform
claims. Reference approval records without copying secrets or restricted data.
